A paid hop, on Base

An outside wallet funded escrow, bought a capability, and took the remainder refund on-chain. Verify it yourself on Basescan.

  1. Deposit External depositor 0x6E94… locked $1 USDC in escrow — openChannel.
  2. Invoke Hub credited the channel and served skopos.security.posture@v1 at $0.08 — off-chain at modelmarket.dev.
  3. Debit Hub metered $0.08 on-chain with the depositor’s signature — debitChannel.
  4. Settle $0.08 to the hub, $0.92 refund to the depositor — settleChannel.

Escrow on Base: 0x0606983c…72C25D. Full table in the on-chain journal §3j. This is buyer settlement proof — not a provider payout promise.
